# Design house rules

Copy this file into your project (root, or next to your CLAUDE.md) and fill it in. The
`world-class-design` skill reads it in Stage 0 and passes it to every agent. It is the only
project-specific input in the whole system.

Delete the guidance in brackets as you go. Mark anything genuinely unconstrained as **open**:
an agent handed a fixed palette designs inside it, an agent handed "open" explores. Locking down
more than the project needs leaves the discovery stage nowhere to go.

---

**Audience:**
[Who they are, and the one thing about them that constrains the design. Not a persona document,
one honest sentence. "Enterprise security engineers who live in a terminal and distrust anything
that looks like marketing."]

**Devices:**
[What they are actually holding, on what connection. This decides whether motion and heavy
imagery are free or expensive.]

**Language:**
[Which language, what reading level, what stays untranslated.]

**Vocabulary:**
[The words you use and the words you never use. Most products have one word for the person and
one for the core unit of the thing. Both matter, everywhere, including button labels.]

**Tone:**
[How it should sound, and the registers that are off-limits.]

**Palette:**
[Fixed hex values, or "open".]

**Type:**
[Fixed families and scale, or "open". Any minimum body size.]

**Design system:**
[What must be reused rather than reinvented, with a path. Or "none, greenfield".]

**Accessibility:**
[Contrast floor, minimum type size, tap target size.]

**Never:**
[Claims, framings, or references the product must not make. Things it must not imply about the
person reading it.]
