# AI design tells and their alternatives

Every model has patterns it overuses. None of these are wrong on their own. They become tells
because users now see them in every AI-made design.

**Do not ban these in the brief.** Banning them up front makes the model overthink and invent
stranger patterns. Work through this list during the Stage 3 refine pass instead: name the
pattern, try the alternative, keep whichever is actually better.

## The four from the source

| Overused pattern | Better alternative |
|---|---|
| Eyebrow text: small redundant labels above a headline that state the obvious | Nine times out of ten, delete it and lose nothing |
| Generic background gradients | A real background image, a texture or pattern, or an honest solid colour |
| Excessive cards and containers, everything boxed | Flatter layouts: grids, tiles, alignment and whitespace doing the work |
| Too many fonts, text styles and typographic levels | 1-2 fonts, 2-4 styles until you genuinely need more. Accent colours and italics sparingly |

## Extended checklist

Composition
- Everything centred, everything evenly spaced, one rhythm the whole way down
- Three-column feature row with an icon, a bold noun, and two lines of body under each
- The hero that is always text-left, CTA below, graphic-right
- Sections that all take the same vertical space regardless of importance

Colour and surface
- Purple, indigo and blue-violet as the default brand colour
- Gradient text on headlines
- Glow and bloom behind cards, buttons and progress bars
- Glassmorphism where nothing is behind the glass
- Five accent colours where one would do

Type
- Inter, Roboto or system-sans as a display face
- Random words bolded or coloured mid-sentence for emphasis
- Body copy set larger than it needs to be, tracking untouched
- Headlines that never risk being smaller or bigger than "medium-large"

Content and chrome
- Decorative icons next to every label
- Emoji used as iconography
- Badges and pills on things nobody needs to scan
- Fake stats, fake logos, fake testimonials filling space
- Empty states that explain themselves in three sentences
- Custom buttons and text fields that look worse than the platform's native controls

Copy (see design-copy-editor)
- "Seamlessly", "effortlessly", "unlock", "elevate", "supercharge", "your journey"
- Em dashes everywhere
- The triple: "It's fast. It's simple. It's yours."
- A subhead that restates the headline in more words

## How to use it

Go pattern by pattern with the actual design in front of you. For each hit: try the alternative,
render both, keep the better one. Report which you tried and which you kept, so the choice is
visible and can be argued with.
